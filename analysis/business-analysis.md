# Business analysis

Status: research framework in progress; no final conclusions.

## Research questions

- How do product sales and recurring services generate revenue and retain customers?
- What evidence supports ecosystem switching costs and product replacement behavior?
- Which product and geographic segments explain growth and concentration?
- Which disclosed indicators can test monetization, adoption and competition?

## Evidence to collect

Reconciled product/geographic revenue tables, management disclosures, segment definitions, competitive evidence and dated source locations. Customer lifetime value, cross-product conversion and retention must remain unquantified if reliable public data is unavailable.

## Working hypothesis

The ecosystem may support additional monetization beyond device replacements. This is a hypothesis to test, not an established finding. Connect each proposed driver to observable revenue, profitability or cash flow evidence.
