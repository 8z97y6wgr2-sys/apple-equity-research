# Profitability and capital efficiency

Status: definitions and work plan; sourced financial calculations pending.

## Measures

- NOPAT = adjusted operating profit × (1 - applicable operating tax rate).
- ROIC = NOPAT / average invested capital. Document treatment of excess cash, goodwill, leases and buybacks.
- WACC = equity weight × cost of equity + debt weight × pretax cost of debt × (1 - tax rate). Use valuation-date market-value weights and a consistent currency.
- EVA = NOPAT - WACC × average invested capital, using the same capital definition as ROIC.
- FCFF = NOPAT + noncash operating charges - capital expenditure - change in operating working capital, with adjustments explained.
