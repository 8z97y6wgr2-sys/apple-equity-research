# Investment thesis

Status: draft framework. Recommendation and target price are pending.

## Thesis to test

Can ecosystem monetization, recurring revenue and capital efficiency sustain cash generation under different product-demand and geographic scenarios?

## Required evidence before conclusion

1. Reconciled historical fundamentals and sourced business analysis.
2. Consistent ROIC, WACC, EVA and FCF calculations.
3. Bear, base and bull operating forecasts with explicit reinvestment assumptions.
4. DCF and relative valuation, including dilution and enterprise-to-equity reconciliation.
5. Valuation-date share price and margin-of-safety assessment.
6. Counterarguments, risks, catalysts and clear conditions that would invalidate the thesis.

Do not infer that past recovery guarantees future recovery. Distinguish business quality from the attractiveness of a particular market price.
