# Risks and catalysts

Status: research register; likelihood, magnitude and evidence not yet evaluated.

| Area to investigate | Evidence needed | Valuation channel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product demand and replacement cycles | Dated revenue and product disclosures | Revenue and margins |
| Geographic exposure, including China | Segment disclosures and relevant primary evidence | Revenue and scenario risk |
| Services regulation and distribution economics | Official decisions and company disclosures | Margin and monetization |
| Competition and product innovation | Documented competitive/product developments | Growth and reinvestment |
| Capital allocation | Cash flow, debt, repurchases and share-count records | Equity bridge and per-share value |

A catalyst must identify an observable event and a mechanism that could change the thesis. A risk must identify how it affects the modeled business. Neither belongs in a recommendation until its evidence and scenario impact are recorded.
